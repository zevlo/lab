# CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 Resources

All facts about the exam itself were verified against CompTIA's official pages on October 2, 2026.

## Knowledge

- [Book: *CompTIA Security+ Get Certified Get Ahead: SY0-701* — Darril Gibson (owned, print + practice exams)](https://www.gcgapremium.com/)
  The primary reading source. Written in plain language, one objective at a time. Use for: first pass on every domain, end-of-chapter questions, and the "review questions" the user does outside this workspace.
- [Video course: Professor Messer SY0-701 — 121 videos, 15h 11m, free](https://www.professormesser.com/security-plus/sy0-701/sy0-701-video/sy0-701-comptia-security-plus-course/)
  Sections map one-to-one onto the exam objectives. Use for: the recommended primary source at the end of each lesson; short targeted videos when a concept does not stick from reading.
- [Official exam objectives PDF — CompTIA (free download)](https://www.comptia.org/training/resources/exam-objectives)
  The canonical list of everything testable. Use for: checking whether a topic is in scope; the exhaustive acronym list in the appendix.
- [Official exam page: Security+ V7 / SY0-701 — CompTIA](https://www.comptia.org/en/certifications/security/v7/)
  Exam facts: max 90 questions, 90 minutes, passing score 750 on a 100–900 scale, mix of multiple choice and performance-based questions. English version retires June 11, 2027. Use for: exam logistics, DoD 8140 work-role mapping.

## Wisdom (Communities)

  Active, moderated certification community. Use for: exam-day experience reports, what "passing difficulty" actually feels like, PBQ surprises.
