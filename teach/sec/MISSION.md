# Mission: Pass CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 (DoD 8140 requirement)

## Why
Passing the SY0-701 exam satisfies a DoD 8140 requirement for a government/contractor security role. The certification is the gate; the deadline is real: the exam must be passed by roughly mid-November 2026.

## Success looks like
- Book the exam in week 1 for a date on or near November 12–13, 2026
- Score 750+ on a timed, full-length practice exam by end of week 5 (Nov 6)
- Classify any scenario the exam describes using correct security vocabulary, without notes
- Pass the real exam on the first attempt

## Constraints
- Six-week sprint starting October 2, 2026
- About 40 study hours per week total; at least 10 hours per week in this workspace
- Background: DevOps (strong on cloud, IaC, CI/CD, automation, change management)
- Primary book already owned: *CompTIA Security+ Get Certified Get Ahead: SY0-701* (Darril Gibson), including practice exams
- All teaching materials use ISO 24495-1:2023 plain language

## Out of scope
- Other certifications (Network+, CySA+, PenTest+)
- Deep cryptography mathematics beyond what the exam requires
- Building a home lab platform beyond quick demonstrations
