# Mission: DevOps Engineering

## Why
Over the next six months, turn broad but basic exposure to DevOps tools into durable production-operating skill, strong enough to compete for DevOps or SRE roles in the United States remote market and Pennsylvania.

## Success looks like
- Build and operate a production-style service on AWS from reviewed commit through automated build, immutable artifact, infrastructure as code, deployment, and observable runtime.
- Diagnose Linux, networking, delivery, cloud, and application failures in a deliberate evidence-first sequence.
- Explain architecture and operational tradeoffs in employer-style technical interviews without relying on memorized tool recipes.
- Present a portfolio repository with diagrams, tests, runbooks, rollback and recovery procedures, and a blameless incident report.

## Constraints
- Sustain 3–5 focused hours per week.
- Prefer deep fundamentals and production reasoning over collecting more tools.
- Prioritize recurring skills in current US remote and Pennsylvania job descriptions; revisit the market evidence as it changes.
- Use AWS as the primary cloud and make cost visibility and teardown part of every cloud lab.

## Out of scope
- Multi-cloud breadth before the AWS capstone is reliable.
- Certification-specific study unless it later supports the hiring goal.
- Adding tools solely because a roadmap lists them.
