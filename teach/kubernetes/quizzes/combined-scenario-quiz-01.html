Combined Scenario Quiz 01

Twelve incidents, no cluster — from memory.

You've closed lessons 0001–0020. This quiz stitches concepts across arcs: troubleshooting funnel, probes, networking, rollouts, Helm, Kustomize, resources, storage, and StatefulSets. Each stem is a mini incident — pick the best first move or root cause, not the fastest-sounding kubectl incantation.

Probe mnemonic (still worth drilling): readiness = membership (quarantine, 0/1, rollout stall) · liveness = existence (restart, RESTARTS climb). When in doubt, readiness only.
