The interview path

This page is the order of study for a Lockheed Martin Space DevSec Engineering Level 2 interview. It keeps the skills that carry the posting, and it leaves the rest until you ask.

What is the order?

Each step uses the one before it. Do the step marked “You are here.” When you pass its check on the first try, the next session moves the mark forward.

  1. Git You are here Basic qualification. The record of every change.
  2. Linux and Bash Desired skills. Files, permissions, a process, then a short script.
  3. A container An image, a container, and a pod are three different things.
  4. Kubernetes Basic qualification. Pod, Deployment, Service, and who can do what. OpenShift is a short note on this step: Kubernetes plus extra admission rules.
  5. Helm Desired skill. A chart is a packaged Kubernetes configuration.
  6. Ansible Basic qualification. Inventory, play, and task. A playbook brings a host to a known state.
  7. Terraform Desired skill. Terraform creates infrastructure. Ansible configures what is already there. Examples use AWS.
  8. A pipeline GitLab CI. Build, test, scan, then a gate that can stop the change.
  9. Argo CD Git is the source of truth. The cluster is made to match it.
  10. Threats and risk One small system, one threat model, then a ranking by mission impact.
  11. Program words Pipeline gate, hardened image, and authority to operate. Public DoD documents and NIST SP 800-218.

What is left for later?

These appear in the posting. They wait until you ask, because they return less for the time you have.

  • OpenStack.
  • A separate Azure course. AWS is the one concrete cloud on this path. Identity, network, and least privilege transfer.
  • A separate OpenShift course. Step 4 carries the short note.
  • Application-language depth beyond reading and changing a short script.
  • A full authorization package, a control-by-control STIG, or a certification exam such as the CKA.

There is no public Lockheed Martin question list. The lessons practice the skills the posting names. Sources are listed in RESOURCES.md.
