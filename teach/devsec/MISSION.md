# Mission: Lockheed Martin DevSec Engineering, Level 2

## Why
Pass a Level 2 technical interview for DevSec Engineering at Lockheed Martin Space. The posting lists many tools. The interview will turn on a short list: Git, Linux and Bash, Kubernetes, Helm, Ansible, Terraform, a pipeline with a security gate, Argo CD, and the judgment to rank a security risk.

## Success looks like
- You can trace one change from a Git commit, through a pipeline gate, onto a Kubernetes cluster.
- You can say which tool does which job: Git, Bash, Helm, Ansible, Terraform, GitLab CI, and Argo CD.
- You can read a small threat and say which risk you would fix first, and why that order matches the mission.
- You can define the program words a Level 2 engineer is expected to use: pipeline gate, hardened image, and authority to operate.

## Constraints
- The horizon is a technical interview in the next few weeks.
- Start at the fundamentals of each skill on the path.
- Speed up when a check is answered cleanly, without help.
- Lessons stay short. One idea, then a check.
- Cloud examples use AWS. The same ideas of identity, network, and least privilege transfer to other clouds.
- Pages follow ISO 24495-1. The reader gets what they need, can find it, can understand it, and can use it.

## Out of scope
- OpenStack, until you ask for it.
- A second cloud course in Azure, or a separate OpenShift course. OpenShift appears only as a short note on Kubernetes.
- Application-language depth beyond reading and changing a short script.
- A full authorization package, a control-by-control STIG, or a certification exam such as the CKA.
- Any claim about a Lockheed Martin interview question list. No public rubric exists.
