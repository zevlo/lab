# Prior experience: dabbler

User runs `docker run` / `docker compose` occasionally and has copy-pasted Dockerfiles, but does not understand what containers/images are or how the commands map onto them. Depth is command-familiarity without mental model.

## Evidence
Self-reported in mission interview (session 1, 2026-10-05).

## Implications
- Don't teach `docker run` syntax from scratch — teach the *model* behind commands they already half-know
- Lesson 0001 targets exactly this gap: image vs container, ephemerality, "container = isolated process"
- Skip nothing in fundamentals vocabulary (image, container, registry, tag) — they may recognize words but can't yet define them
