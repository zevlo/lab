Curriculum & Lesson Plan

AI Inference / Infrastructure Engineering — depth-first. Updated 2026-06-24.

How to read this. Nine stages, taught at depth rather than speed (see MISSION.md). Each stage is broken into small, focused lessons — one tangible win each, in your zone of proximal development. We follow the order top-to-bottom, but it's a living plan: I'll re-sequence based on what you find hard or interesting. Every lesson is hands-on where the 6 GB A2000 allows, conceptual where it builds the mental model. done next planned

The machine and the network that feed the GPU. Processes, memory, GPUs, sockets, HTTP, TCP/IP — at depth.

  1. 01Your GPU and the VRAM budgetdoneThe GPU as a compute device; VRAM = weights + KV cache + overhead; the bandwidth ceiling.
  2. 02Run a model on the A2000nextPull a small model with Ollama, watch the VRAM budget fill live in nvidia-smi; theory → breathing.
  3. 03Processes, threads & the schedulerplannedWhat a "worker" really is; context switches; what htop/ps actually show.
  4. 04Virtual memory, pages & the memory hierarchyplannedRAM vs VRAM, paging, mmap, page cache — the OS idea PagedAttention borrows.
  5. 05Sockets, file descriptors & the syscall path of a requestplannedFrom accept() to read()/write(); strace a tiny server.
